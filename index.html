Chromium web browser.

View on GitHub Explore Features

Designed to get out of your way.

Browsers today are bloated with AI assistants, shopping integrations, and forced account syncs. Minium was built on a different philosophy: a browser should be a tool, not a tracker. It’s just you, the web, and a suite of powerful privacy tools running on a tiny Chromium core. Everything is wiped clean once you leave.

🛡️

AdBlock & Anti-Phishing

Built-in integration with multiple ads, tracker and bad domain sources and phishing detection from PhishTank by Cisco Talos.

🔥

Burn All Data

Everything is ephemeral. A single click instantly destroys all cookies, caches, active sessions, and browsing history, in under a fraction of a second.

🚫

Zero Telemetry

Absolutely no background tracking, no data collection, and no forced account syncs or updates. Your browsing data never leaves your local machine.

🪟

Custom UI

A unified dark palette, custom grips, native tab scrolling, dark-scrollbars, and toast notifications to list a few. The whole window design is custom built.

⌨️

Keyboard Driven

Navigate faster with Minium's extensive shortcuts. Jump between tabs, trigger Picture-in-Picture (WIP), or hard-reload without lifting your hands off the keyboard.

🖥️

Power-User Ready

Universal borderless Picture-in-Picture (WIP) for all video platforms, a docked native Developer Tools pane, and auto-injected custom UserScripts and CSS.

Under the Hood

Powered by Python, PySide6, and QtWebEngine, Minium delivers the rendering performance and standards compatibility of modern Chromium without the Google ecosystem overhead. It features an integrated rootless updater, supports native Linux XDG standards, and is fully open-source under the MIT License.
